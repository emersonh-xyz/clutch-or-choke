# Surface brief: Clutch or Choke (single page)

Scope: the whole app, one page (play flow, situation card, results, demo upload). Mode: Experience.
Audience: people evaluating what the clip API can do; in-fiction, CS viewers. Job: play the daily five, try an upload and watch it become playable.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every clutch surfaces like a bubble from clear water; you read the round through glass before it breaks. Refuses the dark esports stats overlay.

OWN-WORLD: Frutiger Aero eco-tech. Sky gradient into clear aqua water with caustic light; thick glossy glass panes with white top highlight; players as glass bubbles; gel orb buttons (leaf green Clutch, sunset orange Choke); white rounded humanist type; chrome-free, no dark mode.

STORY: The visitor sees a real pro frozen in a 1vX, reads the bubbles (clock, bomb, clutcher, opponents in fixed order), makes the call, watches it break. Then drops a demo into the water and watches clips rise.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sky top third with title wordmark and day/progress; clip in a large glass pane centered; at freeze a giant glossy 1vX numeral left, bubbles rising around the pane, two gel orbs centered under it.

FORM: Clear Water, Open Sky (grounded #5), seed b685d33b. Raises: fixed cross-check order (six-pack), one giant numeral (specimen), pipeline shows structure as rising bubbles (coptic).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
